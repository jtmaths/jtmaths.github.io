# A Level Mathematics: Year 2

A free, complete set of notes, worked examples and practice questions for the **second year of
A Level Mathematics**, following the Department for Education's
[GCE AS and A level subject content for mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gce-as-and-a-level-mathematics).

The second year completes the full A Level. It assumes everything in
[A Level Maths Year 1](../alevel-year1/), deepens each pure topic, and adds new ones such as
parametric equations, sequences and series, and numerical methods. Statistics moves on to the
normal distribution, and mechanics adds moments, friction and projectiles.

Use the menu on the left, or the links below, to reach any topic.

## Pure mathematics

* [Proof](proof/) — **A** · proof by contradiction
* [Algebra and Functions](algebra/) — **B** · partial fractions, modulus, composite and inverse functions, combined transformations
* [Parametric Equations](parametric-equations/) — **C** · converting between forms, and modelling with parameters
* [Sequences and Series](sequences-series/) — **D** · sigma notation, arithmetic and geometric series, the general binomial expansion
* [Trigonometry](trigonometry/) — **E** · radians, small angles, reciprocal functions, compound angles, *R* cos(*x* + *α*)
* [Differentiation](differentiation/) — **G** · exponentials and trig, chain, product and quotient rules, implicit and parametric
* [Integration](integration/) — **H** · substitution, by parts, partial fractions and differential equations
* [Numerical Methods](numerical-methods/) — **I** · locating roots, iteration, Newton–Raphson and the trapezium rule
* [Vectors](vectors/) — **J** · vectors in three dimensions

## Statistics

* [Statistics](statistics/) — **L, M, N** · regression, conditional probability and the normal distribution
* [Hypothesis Testing](hypothesis-testing/) — **O** · tests for correlation and for a normal mean

## Mechanics

* [Mechanics](mechanics/) — **Q, R, S** · moments, resolving forces, friction, projectiles and kinematics with vectors

----

## About these pages

Every topic has an **overview** page covering the big picture, the language, the key formulae and
the common mistakes, plus a page for each group of content statements. Each page contains
step-by-step explanations, several fully worked examples, a quick-reference summary, and **ten
practice questions with complete worked solutions** that you can reveal one at a time.

The bold letters are the content areas of the DfE subject content (A Proof, B Algebra and
functions, and so on), which every exam board's specification is built from.

All pages are self-contained HTML files, so they can be downloaded and used offline.

----

Subject content reference: [DfE GCE AS and A level subject content for mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gce-as-and-a-level-mathematics)

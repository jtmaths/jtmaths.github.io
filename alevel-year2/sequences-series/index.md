# Sequences and Series

Lists of numbers that follow a rule, and what happens when you add them up — DfE content area D.
Arithmetic and geometric series give exact formulae for sums, and the binomial expansion is extended to any rational power.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the two families of sequence, sigma notation, the key formulae and the common mistakes

* [Sequences and Sigma Notation](sequences.html) — *n*th terms, recurrence relations, increasing, decreasing and periodic sequences, and Σ notation
* [Arithmetic Sequences and Series](arithmetic.html) — the *n*th term, the sum formula and its proof, and problems in context
* [Geometric Sequences and Series](geometric.html) — the *n*th term, the sum formula, the sum to infinity and modelling with logarithms
* [The General Binomial Expansion](binomial-expansion.html) — (*a* + *bx*)&#8319; for rational *n*, validity, approximations and partial fractions

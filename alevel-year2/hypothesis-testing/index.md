# Hypothesis Testing

The Year 1 method applied to two new situations: testing for correlation and testing the mean of a normal distribution.
These pages cover DfE content area O (statistical hypothesis testing).

* [An Overview](overview.html) — a recap of the language of hypothesis testing and how the two new tests fit together

## The content statements

* [Testing for Correlation](correlation-tests.html) — testing *&rho;* = 0 with one- and two-tailed alternatives, using critical values of *r* or a *p*-value
* [Testing a Normal Mean](normal-mean-tests.html) — using the distribution of the sample mean, N(*&mu;*, *&sigma;*&sup2;/*n*), to test a mean with known *&sigma;*, with critical regions and *p*-values

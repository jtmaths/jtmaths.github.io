# Mechanics

The second year of mechanics takes the forces and motion of Year 1 into two dimensions.
Bodies can now turn as well as slide, forces act at angles, surfaces are rough, and particles fly through the air.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — what Year 2 adds to Year 1 mechanics, the language, the core techniques and the common mistakes

## The content statements

* [Moments](moments.html) — **S** · the turning effect of a force, beams on supports, tilting, and non-uniform rods
* [Resolving Forces and Inclined Planes](resolving-forces.html) — **R** · components, resultants, equilibrium of a particle, smooth slopes and pulleys
* [Friction](friction.html) — **R** · the model *F* &le; *&mu;R*, rough planes, connected particles, and ladders against walls
* [Projectiles](projectiles.html) — **Q** · time of flight, range, maximum height, the trajectory equation and projection from a height
* [Kinematics with Calculus and Vectors](kinematics-vectors.html) — **Q, R** · *suvat* in vector form, variable acceleration with **i** and **j** components, and **F** = *m***a**

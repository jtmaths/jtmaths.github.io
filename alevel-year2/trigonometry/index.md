# Trigonometry

Radians, three new functions, and a toolkit of identities that turns hard equations into easy ones — DfE content area E.
This builds directly on the Year 1 work on trig graphs, identities and equations.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — radians, the full set of identities, how to choose the right tool, and the common mistakes

## The content statements

* [Radians, Arcs and Sectors](radians.html) — radian measure, exact values, arc length, sector and segment areas
* [Small Angle Approximations](small-angles.html) — sin *θ* ≈ *θ*, cos *θ* ≈ 1 − *θ*²/2, tan *θ* ≈ *θ*, and using them
* [Reciprocal and Inverse Functions](reciprocal-inverse.html) — sec, cosec and cot; arcsin, arccos and arctan; the Pythagorean identities
* [Compound and Double Angles](compound-angles.html) — the addition formulae, double angle formulae, exact values and equations
* [The R cos(x + a) Form](harmonic-form.html) — writing *a* cos *θ* + *b* sin *θ* as a single wave, maxima, minima and equations
* [Trigonometric Proof and Modelling](proof-modelling.html) — proving identities, solving equations in radians, and modelling tides, wheels and daylight

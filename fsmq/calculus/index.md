# Calculus

The mathematics of change, and the most important new idea in the qualification.
Differentiation and integration are restricted to *kx*&#8319; with *n* a positive integer or zero, and sums of such terms.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the two rules that do everything, the notation, and the common mistakes

* [CA5–CA7 · Stationary Points](stationary-points.html) — locating them, determining their nature, and sketching the curve
* [Applications · Maximum and Minimum Problems](optimisation.html) — using calculus to find the best design or the largest volume

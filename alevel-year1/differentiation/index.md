# Differentiation

DfE content area G: the mathematics of rate of change.
Year 1 builds the derivative from first principles, then differentiates *x*<sup>*n*</sup> for rational *n* and uses the result for tangents, normals, stationary points and optimisation.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the big idea, the notation, the five core techniques and the common mistakes

## The content statements

* [Differentiation from First Principles](first-principles.html) — gradients of chords, the limit definition, and proofs for *x*&sup2; and *x*&sup3;
* [Differentiating Powers of x](rules.html) — the rule for *kx*<sup>*n*</sup>, rewriting roots and fractions, and the second derivative
* [Tangents and Normals](tangents-normals.html) — equations of tangents and normals, and working backwards from a gradient
* [Stationary Points and Optimisation](stationary-points.html) — increasing and decreasing functions, maxima and minima, gradient graphs and optimisation in context

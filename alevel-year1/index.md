# A Level Mathematics: Year 1

A free, complete set of notes, worked examples and practice questions for the **first year of
A Level Mathematics**, following the Department for Education's
[GCE AS and A level subject content for mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gce-as-and-a-level-mathematics).

The first year covers the AS content, which every exam board (AQA, Edexcel, OCR and OCR MEI)
teaches: about two thirds **pure mathematics**, with the remaining third shared between
**statistics** and **mechanics**. It builds directly on Higher tier GCSE (9–1) Mathematics.

Use the menu on the left, or the links below, to reach any topic.

## Pure mathematics

* [Proof](proof/) — **A** · deduction, exhaustion and disproof by counter-example
* [Algebra and Functions](algebra/) — **B, D** · indices, surds, quadratics, inequalities, polynomials, graphs, the binomial expansion
* [Coordinate Geometry](coordinate-geometry/) — **C** · straight lines and circles
* [Trigonometry](trigonometry/) — **E** · sine and cosine rules, graphs, identities and equations
* [Exponentials and Logarithms](exponentials-logarithms/) — **F** · e<sup>x</sup>, log laws, equations and modelling
* [Differentiation](differentiation/) — **G** · first principles, the power rule, tangents, stationary points
* [Integration](integration/) — **H** · indefinite and definite integrals, areas under curves
* [Vectors](vectors/) — **J** · vectors in two dimensions and vector geometry

## Statistics

* [Statistics](statistics/) — **K, L** · sampling, presenting data, measures of location and spread, correlation
* [Probability and Distributions](probability/) — **M, N** · Venn diagrams, independence and the binomial distribution
* [Hypothesis Testing](hypothesis-testing/) — **O** · the language of testing, and binomial tests

## Mechanics

* [Mechanics](mechanics/) — **P, Q, R** · units, motion graphs, *suvat*, variable acceleration and Newton's laws

When you have finished Year 1, carry on with [A Level Maths Year 2](../alevel-year2/).

----

## About these pages

Every topic has an **overview** page covering the big picture, the language, the key formulae and
the common mistakes, plus a page for each group of content statements. Each page contains
step-by-step explanations, several fully worked examples, a quick-reference summary, and **ten
practice questions with complete worked solutions** that you can reveal one at a time.

The bold letters are the content areas of the DfE subject content (A Proof, B Algebra and
functions, and so on), which every exam board's specification is built from.

All pages are self-contained HTML files, so they can be downloaded and used offline.

----

Subject content reference: [DfE GCE AS and A level subject content for mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gce-as-and-a-level-mathematics)

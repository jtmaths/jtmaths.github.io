# Coordinate Geometry

Algebra meets geometry: describing lines and circles by equations, and answering geometric questions by solving them.
This is DfE content area **C** (Coordinate geometry in the (*x*,&nbsp;*y*) plane).

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the big idea, the language, the core techniques and the common mistakes

## The content statements

* [Straight Lines](straight-lines.html) — *y*&nbsp;&minus;&nbsp;*y*&#8321; = *m*(*x*&nbsp;&minus;&nbsp;*x*&#8321;), *ax*&nbsp;+&nbsp;*by*&nbsp;+&nbsp;*c*&nbsp;=&nbsp;0, parallel and perpendicular lines, midpoints, distances and straight-line models
* [Circles](circles.html) — (*x*&nbsp;&minus;&nbsp;*a*)&sup2; + (*y*&nbsp;&minus;&nbsp;*b*)&sup2; = *r*&sup2;, completing the square, circle theorems in coordinates, tangents and the discriminant

# Proof

How mathematicians know that something is true for *every* case, not just the ones they have tried.
Proof runs through the whole A Level, and questions may ask you to prove a result in any topic.

* [Disproof by Counter-example](counter-examples.html) — showing a statement is false with a single case where it fails

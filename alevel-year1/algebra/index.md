# Algebra and Functions

The toolkit for every other topic in the course: manipulating expressions, solving equations and
inequalities, sketching and transforming graphs, and expanding brackets with the binomial theorem.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the big idea, the language, the core techniques and the common mistakes

## The content statements

* [Indices and Surds](indices-surds.html) — the laws of indices, including negative and fractional powers; simplifying surds and rationalising denominators
* [Quadratic Functions](quadratics.html) — graphs, the discriminant, completing the square, and quadratics in a function of *x*
* [Simultaneous Equations](simultaneous-equations.html) — elimination and substitution, one linear and one quadratic, and what the solutions mean graphically
* [Linear and Quadratic Inequalities](inequalities.html) — solving inequalities, set notation, and shading regions on a graph
* [Polynomials and the Factor Theorem](polynomials.html) — expanding, factorising, simple algebraic division and the factor theorem
* [Curve Sketching and Proportion](curve-sketching.html) — cubics, quartics, reciprocal graphs, asymptotes, and direct and inverse proportion
* [Transformations of Graphs](transformations.html) — stretches and translations of *y* = f(*x*), and simple combinations
* [The Binomial Expansion](binomial-expansion.html) — expanding (*a* + *bx*)&#8319; for a positive integer *n*, Pascal's triangle and approximations

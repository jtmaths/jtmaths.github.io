# Trigonometry

Sine, cosine and tangent for any angle: solving triangles, sketching graphs, proving identities and solving equations.
This is DfE content area **E** (Trigonometry). In Year 1 all angles are in degrees — radians arrive in Year 2.

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the big idea, the language, the core techniques and the common mistakes

## The content statements

* [Sine and Cosine Rules](sine-cosine-rules.html) — the sine rule and the ambiguous case, the cosine rule, area = &frac12;*ab*&nbsp;sin&nbsp;*C* and bearings
* [Trigonometric Graphs](trig-graphs.html) — sin, cos and tan for any angle, symmetry, periodicity, exact values and transformations
* [Trigonometric Identities](identities.html) — tan&nbsp;&theta; &equiv; sin&nbsp;&theta;&nbsp;/&nbsp;cos&nbsp;&theta;, sin&sup2;&theta; + cos&sup2;&theta; &equiv; 1 and proving identities
* [Trigonometric Equations](equations.html) — solving in a given interval, quadratics in sin or cos, and equations such as sin(*n*&theta;&nbsp;+&nbsp;&alpha;)&nbsp;=&nbsp;*k*

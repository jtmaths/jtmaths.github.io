# Integration

Differentiation in reverse — and, through the fundamental theorem of calculus, the way to find exact areas under curves.
At AS level you integrate *x*&#8319; for rational *n* &ne; &minus;1 and sums of such terms (DfE content area H).

* [An Overview](overview.html) — the big idea, the language, the core techniques and the common mistakes

* [Definite Integrals and Areas](definite-integrals.html) — the fundamental theorem, evaluating definite integrals, regions below the axis and areas between a curve and a line
